Privacy, in plain words.

Tolstoy is built so that there is nothing to leak. This page is short on purpose, and it says the same thing as the code.

What Tolstoy reads

The posts already loaded on x.com while you are looking at the page. That is all. It does not read your other tabs, your history, your messages, or anything you type. Post text is handed to the on-device model, which runs in this same browser on this computer. Handles and post ids are not handed to it, and nothing is logged.

What it stores

Only your settings, and only in this browser: the temperature, whether heated posts are blurred instead of moved, and whether the optional stronger model is present. Nothing about your feed is stored, not the posts, not the handles, not the counts. The small model that Tolstoy reads tone with is part of the extension itself and is not stored anywhere. The stronger model's files live in this browser's own cache, about 110 MB of them, and the Remove link in the panel deletes them. Removing Tolstoy removes the settings and the model.

What it sends

Nothing about your feed. There is no Tolstoy server, no account, no analytics, and no user id. Scoring happens in the page, on this computer, and the list of posts that moved is forgotten when you close the tab.

There is one exception, and you have to agree to it. The optional stronger model is downloaded once from Hugging Face when you press Download, and it is about 110 MB. That download sends Hugging Face nothing but the request for the file, the way any download works. The model is then read from this computer's cache, and it never sends anything back. If you never press Download, the extension makes no network request at all.

Search

The search bar on Tolstoy Home hands your words to your own default search engine, exactly the way the address bar does. That request goes to your engine, not to us.

What each permission is for

  • storage remembers your settings between visits. Settings only.
  • search lets the search bar use your own search engine.
  • Access to x.com lets the calm feed read the timeline you are already looking at, and move posts down. It is the only site Tolstoy touches.
  • offscreen runs the on-device model in a hidden extension page, either the small one that comes with Tolstoy or the stronger one if it has been downloaded. It is how the model keeps working while you scroll, and it reads nothing on its own.

Nothing is deleted

Heated posts are moved down or softened, never removed. Every one of them is listed under “Show them”, with the reason it moved, and a link back to the post itself.
