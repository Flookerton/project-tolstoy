Privacy, in plain words.

Tolstoy is built so that there is nothing to leak. This page is short on purpose, and it says the same thing as the code.

What Tolstoy reads

The posts already loaded on x.com while you are looking at the page. That is all. It does not read your other tabs, your history, your messages, or anything you type.

What it stores

Only your settings, and only in this browser: the temperature, and whether heated posts are blurred instead of moved. Nothing about your feed is stored, not the posts, not the handles, not the counts. Removing Tolstoy removes the settings.

What it sends

Nothing. There is no Tolstoy server, no account, no analytics, and no user id. The extension makes no network request of its own. Scoring happens in the page, on this computer, and the list of posts that moved is forgotten when you close the tab.

Search

The search bar on Tolstoy Home hands your words to your own default search engine, exactly the way the address bar does. That request goes to your engine, not to us.

What each permission is for

  • storage remembers your settings between visits. Settings only.
  • search lets the search bar use your own search engine.
  • Access to x.com lets the calm feed read the timeline you are already looking at, and move posts down. It is the only site Tolstoy touches.

Nothing is deleted

Heated posts are moved down or softened, never removed. Every one of them is listed under “Show them”, with the reason it moved, and a link back to the post itself.

You can check all of this

The rules Tolstoy scores with are in one short file, and this page says the same thing as the code. Read every line of it on GitHub.
