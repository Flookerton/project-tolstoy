About that warning

When you installed Tolstoy, your browser said it can “read and change your data” on sites like x.com. That sounds alarming, so here is what it means.

  1. Read lets Tolstoy see the posts already on your screen, so it can tell which ones are heated.
  2. Change lets it move those posts down or soften them. It never posts, likes, or sends anything for you.
  3. Your browser may also ask if you want to keep Tolstoy’s new tab page. Choose Keep it to see Tolstoy Home.
